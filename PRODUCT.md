# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers returning to work they previously did with supported AI coding CLIs. They may remember only a fragment of the task, project, file, or outcome and need the product to reconstruct what happened without requiring them to understand session-management mechanics.

## Product Purpose

Context Workspace helps a developer answer: “I worked with an AI coding assistant on something before. I do not remember exactly where we left off. Tell me what happened and help me continue.”

Success means the developer can search naturally, recognize the right session, understand the goal, completed work, stopping point, and next action, then resume with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, reassuring.

The interface should reduce uncertainty, communicate clearly, and feel dependable rather than busy or managerial.

## Anti-references

- Dashboard clutter with competing widgets and metrics
- Kanban-first or project-management-first navigation
- Dense operational chrome that obscures the continuity story
- Search controls that require users to understand internal data categories
- Decorative cards, excessive badges, and status indicators without clear user value

## Design Principles

1. Start with recall. Search and recent work are the primary entry points.
2. Tell the continuity story. Show what the work was, what happened, where it stopped, and what to do next.
3. Make continuation obvious. Every useful session view should lead naturally to one clear resume action.
4. Hide management complexity. Projects, tasks, metrics, and integrations are secondary details revealed only when needed.
5. Prefer recognition over configuration. Use normal search across session titles, summaries, projects, working folders, and worked-on files without requiring search scopes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide basic accessibility: semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, meaningful labels, and status information that does not rely only on color.
